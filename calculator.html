FAQ

Fast answers to common TRT calculator questions
How is “dose per injection” calculated? ›

If you choose Inject X days, the calculator converts your weekly total into a per-injection dose using: weekly total ÷ (7 / days-between-injections).

What does “vial strength” mean? ›

Vial strength is concentration (mg per mL). Example: 250 mg/mL means every 1.00 mL contains 250 mg.

Why does syringe size matter? ›

Syringe size helps you sanity-check volume (mL) against what fits comfortably/accurately. It does not change mg.

Can I use this for testosterone cypionate / enanthate? ›

Yes — the math is the same. What changes is your prescribed schedule and concentration.

Why is my per-injection volume tiny or large? ›

Volume depends on concentration. Higher mg/mL = smaller volume for the same mg. Lower mg/mL = larger volume.
