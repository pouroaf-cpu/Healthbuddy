Calculator FAQ

How do I use the calculator?
Set your vial strength (mg/mL), your prescribed total per week (mg/week), and how many injections you do per week. The cards show dose per injection and the mL to draw. The syringe visual updates in real time.
What does “vial strength (mg/mL)” mean?
It’s the concentration printed on your vial label (e.g., 200 mg/mL). The calculator uses this to convert mg to mL.
How do I change syringe size?
Use the “1 mL” or “3 mL” toggle at the top of the inputs. The visual and tick marks adjust to that capacity.
Why do I see “Volume exceeds syringe capacity”?
Your per-injection volume is larger than the selected syringe can hold. Choose a larger syringe or increase injections per week so each shot is smaller.
What’s the difference between mL and insulin “units”?
Insulin syringes often mark “units” where 100 units = 1 mL. This tool displays volume in mL; if your syringe is U-100, multiply mL × 100 to get units (e.g., 0.25 mL = 25 units).
Can I split my weekly dose differently?
Yes. Change “Injections per week” to your schedule (e.g., 2×/week, 3×/week). The tool recalculates the per-injection mL automatically.
Does this tell me what dose I should take?
No. It converts a prescribed weekly dose into per-injection volume. Always follow your clinician’s instructions.
Does the calculator save my settings?
The syringe size and last inputs may be stored locally in your browser so you don’t have to re-enter them. Clearing site data resets this.
Does this send any data to a server?
The calculation runs in your browser. If you’ve enabled analytics (e.g., Microsoft Clarity), page usage may be tracked, but the calculator inputs aren’t sent by this tool.
Which browsers and devices are supported?
Modern Chromium, Firefox, and Safari on mobile and desktop. If the sliders look odd, update your browser or try another device.
