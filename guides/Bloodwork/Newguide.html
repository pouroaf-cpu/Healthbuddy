How to calculate testosterone dosage with this calculator

The logic is simple: total weekly dose → dose per injection → volume in mL. The calculator does the maths for you, but here’s what happens under the hood.

1. Start with your prescribed weekly dose

Enter the total amount of testosterone your prescriber has recommended per week (for example, 140 mg/week of testosterone cypionate). This is the baseline the testosterone dosage calculator works from.

2. Choose how often you inject

Select how many times per week you inject. The TRT calculator divides your total weekly dose by this number to give a clear mg amount per shot. No spreadsheets or manual division needed.

3. Convert mg to mL based on vial strength

Finally, you tell the tool your vial strength — for example a common testosterone cypionate 200 mg/mL vial. The calculator then converts the mg dose into mL, acting as a simple testosterone injection calculator so you know exactly how far to pull the plunger.

4. Apply it consistently

Once you know your mg and mL per injection, you can repeat the same volume every time. That makes it easier to keep your protocol consistent so your bloodwork and symptoms aren’t all over the place.

Common questions about this TRT calculator

Is this testosterone dosage calculator only for TRT?

It’s designed as a TRT calculator, meaning it assumes medical supervision and a clear weekly dose from a prescriber. The maths is the same regardless, but this tool is not a recommendation to start or change treatment on your own.

Does it support different vial strengths of testosterone cypionate?

Yes. As long as you enter the correct mg/mL strength, the calculator effectively behaves as a testosterone cypionate calculator. Common examples include 100 mg/mL and 200 mg/mL vials.

Can I use this as a general testosterone injection calculator?

Yes. The tool simply converts your total weekly dose and injection frequency into a per-shot amount in mg and mL. That makes it a straightforward testosterone injection calculator for most standard injectable testosterone protocols.

Does this calculator tell me what dose I should be on?

No. It only helps you calculate testosterone dosage once a medical professional has set your prescription. It does not suggest, optimise, or approve any specific dose.

Not medical advice

This testosterone dosage calculator is for general educational use only. It does not interpret your bloodwork, set your treatment plan, or replace advice from a qualified professional. Always confirm any changes to dose, schedule, or medication with your prescriber.
