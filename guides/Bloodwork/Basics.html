Bloodwork Basics for TRT

A practical breakdown of the blood tests that actually matter when you're on TRT.

Why bloodwork matters on TRT

TRT affects more than testosterone. Bloodwork is the safety net that keeps your protocol stable.

The core labs you should monitor

Core panel

  • Total Testosterone
  • Free Testosterone
  • SHBG
  • Estradiol (Sensitive)
  • CBC
  • CMP
  • Lipid Panel

Situational

  • Prolactin
  • TSH / T3 / T4
  • PSA
  • HbA1c

When to test

  • Before starting TRT
  • 6–8 weeks after changes
  • Every 6–12 months once stable

Key takeaways

  • Bloodwork closes the loop
  • Consistency matters more than frequency
  • Numbers guide decisions — not feelings alone
