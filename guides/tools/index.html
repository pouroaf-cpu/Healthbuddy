Why these tools exist

Most dosing mistakes come from unit confusion and mental math. These tools standardise the calculations so you can focus on execution.

Principle

If the math is wrong, the dose is wrong.

Common calculator mistakes

  • Confusing mg and mcg
  • Entering the wrong vial strength
  • Entering the wrong water amount (mL) after mixing
  • Using the wrong syringe scale
  • Changing dilution mid-vial without recalculating
Pattern

Bad inputs produce confident-looking wrong outputs.
