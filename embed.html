Embed the InjectBuddy Calculator

You can add the InjectBuddy TRT dosage calculator to your own website with a simple iframe. Your visitors stay on your page while using our calculator, and the tool still links back to InjectBuddy.

Standard Embed (Recommended)

Copy and paste this snippet into any page where you want the calculator to appear:

<iframe src="https://www.injectbuddy.com/embed/calculator.html" width="100%" height="960" style="border:0;border-radius:12px;box-shadow:0 0 20px rgba(0,0,0,.10);" loading="lazy"> </iframe>

This uses the embedded version of the TRT calculator, optimised for iframes. The footer includes a small credit link back to InjectBuddy.

  • Width: set to 100% so it fits whatever container you use.
  • Height: 960 works for most layouts (increase if your page feels cramped).
  • Styling: you can remove the shadow / radius if you want it to blend into your own design.

Minimal Embed (No Extra Styling)

If you prefer to control all styling yourself, use the minimal version:

<iframe src="https://www.injectbuddy.com/embed/calculator.html" width="100%" height="900" style="border:0;" loading="lazy"> </iframe>

You can wrap this in your own card, column, or section and apply your own borders, spacing, and shadows.

Basic Usage Notes

  • The calculator runs entirely inside the iframe – no extra scripts are required on your side.
  • All updates and improvements happen on InjectBuddy’s side without you needing to change the embed code.
  • We do not store or track individual dose inputs. See the privacy policy for full details.
  • If your site uses a very narrow mobile layout, you may want to increase the iframe height to avoid scroll bars.

If you embed InjectBuddy on a public site, a visible link back (for example under the iframe) is appreciated, but the embedded version already includes a small credit link by default.
