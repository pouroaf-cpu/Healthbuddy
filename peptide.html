Peptide FAQ

What does mg/mL mean?
It’s the concentration after reconstitution. Example: 5 mg in 2 mL = 2.5 mg/mL (2,500 µg/mL).
How do I get units for an insulin syringe?
On U-100 syringes, 1 mL = 100 units. Multiply mL × 100. Example: 0.12 mL = 12 units.
Why do I get a capacity warning?
Your per-dose mL exceeds the chosen syringe size. Pick 3 mL or reduce the per-dose amount.
