Privacy Policy

InjectBuddy is designed to be simple and private. We do not sell your data, and the calculators are built so you can use them without creating an account or entering personal identifiers.

Who We Are

InjectBuddy provides free tools to help people understand TRT and peptide dosing math. The site is informational and educational only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you have questions about this policy you can contact the site owner via the Contact page.

What Data We Collect (Short Answer: Very Little)

The core calculators (TRT dosage, peptide reconstitution, BMI, and related tools) run in your browser. When you move sliders or enter values, that data is calculated locally and is not sent to a backend dosing database owned by InjectBuddy.

Information you actively provide

  • If you use the Contact form or email us directly, we will see whatever you choose to send (for example, your name, email address, and your message).
  • This information is used only to respond to your query and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, some technical information is collected automatically for security, performance, and analytics:

  • Basic server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps).
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous analytics data through tools such as Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, or Ahrefs.

This data is used to understand how the site is used (for example, which pages are most popular) so that we can improve layout and performance. We do not use this data to build personal marketing profiles.

Cookies and Analytics

InjectBuddy uses cookies and similar technologies primarily for:

  • Basic site functionality (for example, remembering UI preferences such as mode toggles).
  • Usage analytics (for example, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and/or Ahrefs). These services may set their own cookies.

These third-party services may combine data from InjectBuddy with data from other sites they track. We do not control their independent use of information. You can manage cookies via your browser settings or opt-out of certain analytics via browser add-ons where available.

If you block cookies or scripts, the calculators should still function, but some analytics and UX features may be disabled.

No Dosing History Database (Right Now)

At this time, InjectBuddy does not provide login accounts and does not store a personal history of your doses, lab values, or injection dates on a hosted user profile.

  • No account system for end users is active.
  • No personalised dosing log is stored on our servers.
  • Any values you enter into the calculators are calculated in the browser and cleared when you refresh or leave the page (aside from limited local browser storage used for simple preferences such as last selected mode or syringe size).

If a future version introduces accounts or cloud-saved logs, this privacy policy will be updated and any new feature will clearly explain what is stored and how to control or delete it.

How We Use Your Information

Where we do receive or collect information, we use it for:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Improving calculator design, performance, and content.
  • Responding to questions sent via the contact form or email.
  • Preventing abuse, spam, and security issues.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share contact details with third-party advertisers.

Data Sharing

We may share limited information only in the following cases:

  • Service providers: For example, hosting providers, analytics tools, or error-tracking services that help keep the site running. These providers are expected to handle data only on our behalf and not for their own marketing.
  • Legal reasons: If required by law, regulation, or court order, or to protect our rights, security, or users.

We do not share your calculator inputs or contact messages with third-party advertisers.

Data Retention

Server logs and analytics data are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for security, diagnostics, and trend analysis, and then are deleted or aggregated.

If you contact us directly, emails or contact form submissions may be kept for reference and support history. You can request that we delete previous communication where technically and legally possible.

Children's Privacy

InjectBuddy is aimed at adults. TRT and peptide-related content is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided personal details via the site, please contact us and we will review and remove that information where appropriate.
