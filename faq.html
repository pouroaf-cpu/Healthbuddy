FAQ

Does this replace medical advice?

No. It’s for planning only. Follow your clinician’s guidance.

How do I use the syringe visual?

Calculate the mL per injection, then fill to the shown mark. Toggle between 1 mL and 3 mL syringes to match what you use.

What do U-100 units mean?

U-100 indicates 100 units per mL on insulin syringes. We show a units equivalent to make reading easier.
