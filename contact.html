About the Creator

InjectBuddy was built out of practical necessity. Doses, concentrations, and injection schedules are often scattered across forums and dense clinical documents — not ideal for day-to-day decision making.

It started as a private calculator to reduce mistakes and second-guessing. It has grown into a public resource designed for clarity, accuracy, and privacy.

The objective is straightforward: remove friction, show conversions honestly, and help people feel more in control of their therapy.

Principles

  • Privacy first: No logins required for calculators. Inputs stay in your browser.
  • Accuracy without jargon: Clear units, realistic syringe visuals, transparent math.
  • Continuous improvement: Feedback drives fixes, refinements, and new features.
