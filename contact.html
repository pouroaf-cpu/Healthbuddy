About the Creator

InjectBuddy was built out of personal necessity. Like many navigating hormone therapy, I found the process confusing and inconsistent. Dosages, concentrations, and injection schedules were scattered across forums and dense clinical documents — not practical for day-to-day decisions.

After working through my own health challenges — including the realities of balancing therapy with energy, recovery, and mood — it became clear that simple, precise tools were missing. InjectBuddy started as a private calculator to reduce mistakes and second-guessing. It has since grown into a public resource designed for clarity, accuracy, and privacy.

There is no agenda beyond accessibility. The goal is to remove friction, present conversions honestly, and help people feel more in control of their therapy.

Principles

  • Privacy first: No logins required for calculators. Your inputs stay in your browser.
  • Accuracy without jargon: Clear units, realistic syringe visuals, and transparent math.
  • Continuous improvement: User feedback guides fixes, refinements, and new features.
