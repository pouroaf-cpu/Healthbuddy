BMI Calculator

Estimate your Body Mass Index using your weight and height. Supports metric (kg / cm) and imperial (lb / ft / in).

Standard BMI formula Metric & imperial Instant estimate

How this works:

  • For metric: BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]².
  • For imperial: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) / [height (in)]².
  • Returns a single number and a basic category label.
BMI is a rough population-level index. It does not distinguish muscle from fat and can be misleading for individuals. It does not replace a proper clinical assessment or body composition measurement.
